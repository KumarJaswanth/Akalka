A manufacturer, defined by product.

AKALKA manufactures doors, sandwich panels, cleanroom panels, partition and view glass panels — and the profiles that join them — for clean, finished interiors.

Three things, made to fit together.

Most interiors are assembled from parts that were never meant to meet. AKALKA makes the openings, the surfaces and the junctions as one system.

Openings, walls and junctions that read as one.

Doors, panels and profiles are produced to meet — the same coated finish, the same colours, the same confirmed specification — so a room reads as one continuous surface.

Why AKALKA

  • Made to meet: doors, panels and profiles come from one maker, so junctions line up in the factory instead of being improvised on site.
  • Variation without invention: sizes and colours adapt to requirement. Anything unconfirmed is marked plainly.
  • Finish as discipline: White, Matt White or matched colour across every element, so the whole project stays coherent.

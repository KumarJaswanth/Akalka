Questions, answered straight.

Who are the doors and panels manufacturers in Vizag?
AKALKA Doors & Panels is a manufacturer of doors, sandwich panels, cleanroom panels, partition and view glass panels and profiles, based in Visakhapatnam (Vizag), Andhra Pradesh.
Do you supply outside Visakhapatnam?
Yes. AKALKA supplies projects across Andhra Pradesh and neighbouring states. Share the site location with your enquiry and delivery is confirmed with the specification.
Do you have standard door or panel sizes?
No fixed catalogue sizes — doors and panels are manufactured to requirement. Send your openings or layout and the sizes come back confirmed.
Which panel thicknesses are available?
PUF and rockwool double-skin wall panels are confirmed in 50 mm and 100 mm. Thicknesses for the rockwool and honeycomb sandwich panel types are confirmed on enquiry.
What finishes and colours can I choose?
Pre-powder coated or powder coated, in White, Matt White, or colour as per customer requirement — the same across doors, panels and profiles.
What about fire ratings or cleanroom classifications?
These are not published on the site. Ask in the form — every specific question is answered and confirmed in writing, never assumed.
What should I send with an enquiry?
Drawings, opening schedules, panel layouts, thicknesses, finishes, quantities and the site location. Anything specified beats anything described.
